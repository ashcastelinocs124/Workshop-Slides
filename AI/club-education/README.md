# Club Education — Agentic AI @ UIUC

Lectures and workshops from **Agentic AI @ UIUC**, the student AI club I founded and ran at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**. The club taught **AI, AI agents, and agentic coding with Claude Code** through weekly lectures, hands-on workshops, and semester-long build projects, and grew to 300+ members.

**Source:** [Agentic-AI-UIUC/Spring-Slides](https://github.com/Agentic-AI-UIUC/Spring-Slides)

---

## Background

- **Lectures.** Each week covered one core concept in agentic AI, from the basics of agents through memory, RAG, evals, MCP, and reinforcement learning. Most sessions opened with the latest AI news.
- **Workshops.** In hands-on Claude Code and OpenClaw sessions, members learned by building: agent loops, tools, context management, and autonomous coding workflows.
- **From curious to builder.** Members shipped projects and connected with research labs and startups on campus.
