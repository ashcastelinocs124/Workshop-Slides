# AI Update

Slides for my **weekly AI landscape update** on Substack, published under **ARC Research**. Each week's deck covers what moved in AI: **frontier and open-weight models**, **applications**, **AI chips**, and the **semiconductor** supply chain behind them. It focuses on benchmarks, cost, strategy, and what the news means for markets.

*For educational purposes only — not investment advice.*
