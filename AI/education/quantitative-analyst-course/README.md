# Gies MSBA Workshop Series — AI, ML, and Financial Markets

Lecture slides from a nine-session workshop series I designed and taught for the **Gies College of Business MSBA program** (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Fall 2026). The series takes students from their first AI agent, through the machine-learning foundations behind it, to using both in real investment research — the toolkit of a modern **quantitative analyst**.

---

## 🎯 Background

Quant and research roles increasingly expect analysts to build their own tooling: agents that gather and summarize evidence, ML models that score and forecast, and the judgment to know when neither is the right answer. This series was built to close that gap for business-analytics students who know some Python but have never shipped an agent or a model.

Three ideas shaped the design:

- **One story, start to finish.** Every chapter is set at **Champaign Capital Research**, a fictional independent equity research firm adopting agents and ML one careful step at a time. Students play the firm's **new quantitative analyst**, and each chapter hands them a real task from that job — they do the work rather than watch demos.
- **Hands-on by default.** Each session pairs the slides here with an interactive online textbook: code that runs in the browser, an instant-feedback checkpoint quiz, and a Colab exercise using the campus LLM service. No local setup required.
- **Judgment over tools.** Everything before session 7 is a tool. Session 7 is where students decide which one to reach for — ML, an agent, or neither — before applying it all to financial markets.

Workshop site (interactive textbook): [dl-msba-workshop.azurewebsites.net](https://dl-msba-workshop.azurewebsites.net/intro.html)
